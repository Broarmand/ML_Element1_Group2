# QUESTION 2 – SCENARIO PROPOSAL

## Predicting Road Traffic Accident Severity in Rwanda

Group: Group 2  
Members: Armand Ikirezi and Mariam Nyirarukundo

### 1. Scenario
Road traffic accidents are a road safety concern in Rwanda. Traffic safety stakeholders need information about accident conditions associated with different severity levels to support planning and preventive interventions.

### 2. Problem Statement
The problem to investigate is the classification of road traffic accident severity based on road characteristics, weather conditions, lighting, vehicle type and accident-related characteristics.

### 3. Stakeholder
Rwanda Road Safety and Traffic Management Unit.

### 4. Objective
To prepare a machine-learning-ready dataset containing road traffic accident characteristics and a severity target for future classification.

### 5. Target Variable
`severity`

Classes:
- Minor
- Serious
- Fatal

### 6. Dataset Requirements
The dataset will contain at least 1,000 records, at least eight input features, numerical and categorical variables, and a clearly defined target.

### 7. Proposed Data Source
A reproducibly generated synthetic dataset created by the group using Python. The data will not contain personal identifiers.

### 8. Ethical Considerations and Limitations
The dataset will be explicitly identified as simulated. It will not be presented as actual Rwanda police or road safety statistics. It is intended for educational dataset-preparation practice, not operational traffic safety decisions.

- Cleaning Log
- Feature Justification table
- Model-ready datasets
- Reproducible preprocessing pipeline
